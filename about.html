About KijaniPulse Network

KijaniPulse Network is a student-led environmental initiative founded by Benedict Samwel Mfanga and co-founded by Roque Ricco DeSouza at St. Joseph Boys Science High School, Kilimanjaro, Tanzania.

Our mission is to mobilize students through education, innovation, and action to create real environmental impact. We believe young people can turn green ideas into lasting change in their schools and communities.
